Before you begin

If you have entered or left Canada, this tool can help you determine your residency status for Canadian income tax purposes, which affects your income tax obligations and filing requirements in Canada.

Note: If you have not entered or left Canada but plan to in the future, use this tool at that time to help determine your residency status.

You will need the following information:

  • dates you entered or left Canada
  • details about your home, family, and other personal ties
  • information about your work or finances
  • your plans for living in or outside Canada
info
The residency determination is based on the information you provide. If your situation changes, your residency status may change too. Answer all questions that apply to your situation.

    Specified person

    A "specified person" for GST/HST purposes is a high-volume business or a financial institution. You are classified as a specified person if you are listed as a financial institution, or if your taxable supplies (along with your associates) exceed $6 million in both your current and preceding fiscal years. For more information, go to Excise Tax Act.
